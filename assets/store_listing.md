```
🚀 Supercharge your PhysicsWallah (pw.live) lecture experience!

Enhancer for Physics Wallah gives you complete control over your video playback on the pw.live student portal. Unlock smooth speeds up to 4.0x, pop out floating Picture-in-Picture windows, and remove distracting screen elements in just one click.

Whether you are preparing for JEE, NEET, UPSC, or Board exams, Enhancer for Physics Wallah helps you learn faster, save study time, and stay 100% focused.

--------------------------------------------------
✨ KEY FEATURES
--------------------------------------------------

⚡ UNLOCK HIGH-SPEED PLAYBACK (Up to 4.0x)
• Break past the standard 2.0x video speed limit.
• Smooth slider control from 0.5x all the way up to 4.0x (0.1x step tuning).
• Instant preset buttons: Jump to 1.0x, 2.0x, 3.0x, or 4.0x instantly.
• Mouse Wheel Speed Adjustment: Hover over the player slider and scroll up/down to adjust speed on the fly.

📱 PICTURE-IN-PICTURE (PiP) FLOATING PLAYER
• Pop out your lecture into a floating video window that stays on top of all apps.
• Multitask, solve practice questions, or write notes on your computer while watching.
• Speed controls and custom rates remain active inside PiP mode.

🎯 1-CLICK DISTRACTION REMOVAL
Toggle off clutter to keep your eyes strictly on your teacher's whiteboard:
• Hide Ask AI Bot Capsule
• Hide Q&A Doubt Chat Overlay
• Hide Live Comments & Chat
• Hide Lecture PDF Study Notes
• Hide Seek Timelines & Time Labels

⌨️ CUSTOMIZABLE KEYBOARD HOTKEYS
Control your video without touching your mouse:
• Speed Up (+0.1x): Default 'H'
• Slow Down (-0.1x): Default 'J'
• Reset Speed (1.0x): Default 'L'
• Hold Spacebar to temporarily boost speed (default 2.0x)
```
