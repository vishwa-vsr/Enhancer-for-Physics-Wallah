# Privacy Policy for Enhancer for Physics Wallah

**Effective Date:** August 22, 2026  
**Latest Version:** 1.0.7.5

At **Enhancer for Physics Wallah** (also known as **PW Control**), we believe your privacy should always come first. This Privacy Policy explains clearly and transparently how our browser extension operates and how user data is handled.

---

## 1. Zero Data Collection & Zero Tracking

**We do not collect, store, transmit, track, or sell any of your personal information, browsing history, or lecture activity.**

- **No Analytics or Telemetry:** There are no tracking pixels, analytics scripts (such as Google Analytics), or usage trackers inside this extension.
- **No Account Access:** We do not read, access, or store your Physics Wallah account credentials, passwords, student profile details, test scores, or payment information.
- **No Third-Party Advertising:** The extension contains zero advertisements and does not share any data with advertising networks.

---

## 2. Real-Time Audio Processing (Skip Silence)

The **Skip Silence** feature dynamically fast-forwards through pauses and silence during lectures.

- **How it works:** The extension measures the volume level of the lecture video in real time to detect quiet moments and adjust playback speed.
- **No Recording or Storage:** Lecture audio and video are **never** recorded, saved, downloaded, or uploaded to any server. Audio analysis occurs entirely in temporary browser memory and is discarded instantly.
- **No Microphone Access:** The extension **never** accesses, activates, or listens to your computer microphone.

---

## 3. Local Storage on Your Device

The extension only uses the standard browser local storage API (`chrome.storage.local`) to save your interface preferences directly on your own device:

- Preferred playback speed and custom speed presets.
- Focus mode visibility choices (e.g., hiding live chat, notes, doubts, or batch details).
- UI theme selection (Dark Mode / Light Mode).
- Skip Silence settings (silence speed, silence threshold, minimum pause duration, and auto-calibration preference).
- Accumulated "Time Saved" study counter.

**All stored settings remain strictly on your local device.** They are never uploaded to any remote server or shared with any third party.

---

## 4. Website Permissions & Content Scripts

The extension only activates on official Physics Wallah lecture portals (`pw.live`, `penpencil.co`, `penpencil.xyz`, and `pwnet.in`).

- It runs solely to attach the custom video control bar (speed slider, Picture-in-Picture button, Skip Silence visualizer) and apply Focus Mode decluttering.
- It does not run on or monitor any other websites you visit.

---

## 5. Voluntary Feedback & Uninstall Surveys

- **Feedback Link:** The popup menu includes an optional link to a Google Form where you can submit feedback or feature requests.
- **Uninstall Survey:** When you uninstall the extension, your browser may open an optional exit survey hosted on Google Forms to help us understand why you removed it.
- **Voluntary Participation:** Filling out these forms is completely optional. Any information submitted on Google Forms is governed by [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). The extension itself does not transmit any form data automatically.

---

## 6. Security & No Remote Code

- **100% Local Package:** All executable scripts, styles, and assets are bundled directly within the extension package installed on your computer.
- **No Remote Code Execution:** In full compliance with Chrome Web Store, Mozilla Add-ons, and Microsoft Edge policies, the extension does not download or execute any remote code or external scripts at runtime.
