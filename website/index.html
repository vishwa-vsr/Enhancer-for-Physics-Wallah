Enhancer for PW — Speed Up Your PhysicsWallah Lectures

Free browser extension to watch Physics Wallah lectures at up to 4.0x speed, auto-skip teacher silence, remove chat distractions, and lock HD video quality on pw.live. Available for Chrome, Firefox, and Edge.

Add to Chrome · Add to Firefox · Add to Edge

Features — Supercharge Your PW Study Sessions

Muscle-Memory Hotkeys

Tap H to speed up, J to slow down, L to reset, and hold Space for turbo boost. Never touch your mouse while studying.

Smart Skip Silence

Auto-accelerates through silent board writing and teacher pauses, returning to normal the moment speech resumes. 100% on-device processing.

1-Click Focus Mode

Remove live chat drama, doubt box spam, and AI popups. Leaves only the whiteboard on screen so you can study in peace.

Instant HD Quality Lock

Never wait for blurry video to buffer. Automatically locks your preferred 720p or 1080p resolution.

Real Finish Clock

Calculates the exact wall-clock time your lecture will end at your active speed.

Smart Tab Auto-Pause

Switched tabs to check formula notes? The lecture pauses and resumes the second you switch back.
