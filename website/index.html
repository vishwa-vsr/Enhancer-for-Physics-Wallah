Features — Supercharge Your PW Study Sessions

Skip Silence

Automatically speeds up when the teacher stops talking or writes on the board, and returns to normal the second they speak. 100% on-device processing.

Constant 720p HD Quality

Never wait for blurry video to buffer. Automatically locks crisp 720p HD quality so lectures stay clear from start to finish.

Customizable Keyboard Shortcuts

Control speed with default keys (H to speed up, J to slow down), or customize them to any keys you like in settings.

1-Click Focus Mode

Instantly hide live chat, doubt box, Ask AI, and other on-screen icons so you can focus in peace.

Real Finish Clock

Calculates the exact wall-clock time your lecture will end at your active speed.

Smart Tab Auto-Pause

Switched tabs to check formula notes? The lecture pauses and resumes the second you switch back.
