How to Automatically Skip Silence in Physics Wallah Lectures

Skip Silence is a built-in lecture accelerator in Enhancer for Physics Wallah. When teachers pause to write on the blackboard, solve complex equations in silence, or take sips of water, the extension speeds through the dead air automatically and drops back to your regular speed the millisecond the teacher speaks again.

Key Benefits

  • Save 15 to 25 Minutes: Fast-forward through quiet board-writing and pauses automatically without touching the keyboard.
  • Zero Word Clipping: Instant audio detection restores your standard speed in milliseconds so you never miss explanations.
  • 100% On-Device & Private: Audio analysis runs locally inside your browser window in RAM. No audio data ever leaves your computer.

Why Students Need Silence Skipping

In a typical two-hour batch lecture, up to 20% of the total runtime consists of pauses, silent problem-solving, and blackboard transitions.

  • Eliminate Backlog Burnout: Instead of spending 2.5 hours on a recorded lecture, finish in under 90 minutes with zero loss of comprehension.
  • No Manual 10-Second Skips: Manually tapping the forward button often skips past the beginning of an important derivation or formula. Skip Silence is millisecond-precise.
  • Maintains Flow State: Dead air causes students to check phones or lose attention. Keeping playback snappy keeps your mind locked into the subject.

How to Configure Skip Silence

Open any lecture on pw.live, click the Enhancer extension icon, and open the Media settings:

  1. Enable Skip Silence: Turn on the master switch. The extension will begin monitoring audio levels in real time.
  2. Silence Speed: Adjust your silent playback rate (default is 2.5x). When silence hits, the video accelerates to this speed immediately.
  3. Auto Noise Calibration: Keeps background mic hiss and fan noise from falsely triggering speech detection.

Pro Tip: Quiet Board-Writing Derivations

When teachers solve long physics or math problems silently across multiple blackboards, you can safely bump Silence Speed up to 3.0x to review their steps in seconds.
