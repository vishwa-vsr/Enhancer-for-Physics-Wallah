How to use the Padhle dashboard in Enhancer for Physics Wallah

The Padhle dashboard is a built-in study planner and task manager inside the Enhancer for Physics Wallah extension. It helps you organize your study schedule, track daily practice problems, and plan your chapters in one clean place without opening separate productivity apps or keeping paper notes.

Key Highlights

  • Subject and Chapter Organization: Group your tasks by subject and chapter with custom colors and icons.
  • Smart Due Dates and Backlog Alerts: Schedule work for today or tomorrow, with an overdue section for missed tasks.
  • 100% Private and Offline: All your tasks stay saved right inside your browser. No sign-up or tracking.

1. Setting Up Subjects and Chapters

The dashboard organizes your study material into two tiers:

  • Subjects: Click the plus icon next to Subjects in the left sidebar to add a subject with a custom icon and color (such as Physics, Chemistry, or Mathematics).
  • Chapters: Click on any subject to expand it, then click "Add Chapter" to create individual topics (such as Kinematics or Chemical Bonding).

2. Adding and Scheduling Study Tasks

Click the "Add Task" button in the top right corner of the dashboard or inside any chapter. Enter task details:

  • Task title: What you plan to work on, such as "Watch Lecture 05" or "Complete DPP 03".
  • Subject and chapter: Assign the task to a chapter, or leave it blank to send it to your Inbox.
  • Task Type: Mark as Lecture, DPP, Notes Revision, or Test Prep.
  • Due Date: Pick Today, Tomorrow, or a custom calendar date.

3. List View and Board View

Switch between a clean daily to-do checklist or a Kanban-style board with To Do, In Progress, and Completed columns.
