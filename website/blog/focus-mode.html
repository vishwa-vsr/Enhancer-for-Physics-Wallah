How to Use 1-Click Focus Mode to Remove PW Distractions

1-Click Focus Mode instantly strips away live chat spam, doubts boxes, Ask AI widgets, and floating UI buttons from pw.live, giving you a clean cinema-style lecture display.

Key Benefits

  • Zero Chat Spam: Block out endless emoji streams and irrelevant comments during live and recorded batches.
  • Cinema Screen Layout: Dedicate 100% of your monitor pixels strictly to teacher slides and blackboard diagrams.
  • Auto-Hide Controls: Player buttons fade out smoothly until you deliberately move your mouse.

Why Students Need Focus Mode

Live batches frequently suffer from distracting comments and floating widget clutter that steal your attention.

  • Protect Your Concentration: Watching students debate in live chat pulls your brain away from difficult physics formulas.
  • Maximize Blackboard Real Estate: Removing sidebars lets the video fill your entire display with crisp clarity.
  • Peaceful Recorded Watching: Even on recorded lectures, distracting overlay icons get in the way of problem text.

What Focus Mode Removes

Open the Focus tab inside the Enhancer popup menu on pw.live:

  • Hide Live Chat: Completely eliminates comment streams and reaction popups.
  • Hide Doubt Box & Ask AI: Clears floating assistant buttons that cover parts of the lecture board.
  • Hide Notes Icon: Hides unnecessary badges across the top and side of the video frame.
  • Auto-Hide Controls: The player timeline and buttons vanish after 3 seconds of mouse inactivity.

Pro Tip: Auto-Hide Player Timeline

Turn on "Auto-Hide Controls" in the Focus tab. The timeline and buttons will vanish completely after 3 seconds of mouse inactivity.
