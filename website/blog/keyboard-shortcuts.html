Mastering Keyboard Shortcuts for Physics Wallah Lectures

Keyboard shortcuts allow you to adjust playback speed, rewind tricky derivations, and toggle distraction-free mode without lifting your pen or moving your mouse.

Key Benefits

  • Zero Mouse Distraction: Keep your hands on your notes and keyboard while controlling your lecture smoothly.
  • Precision Speed Tapping: Increment speed by 0.1x steps to find the exact sweet spot for each teacher.
  • Fully Rebindable: Use natural defaults (H / J / L) or customize any key to match your muscle memory.

Why Shortcuts Save Study Time

Reaching for the mouse every few minutes breaks physical posture and interrupts deep focus.

  • Faster Pacing Adjustments: Speed up during simple introductory slides and slow down for complex proofs in a single keystroke.
  • Instant Formula Rewind: Missed a key term? Tap left arrow to jump back 10 seconds without hunting for the timeline.
  • One-Tap Focus: Press F to hide all comment spam the second live class banter gets noisy.

Default Shortcuts & Customization

Access your shortcuts under the Playback tab inside the extension popup:

  • Speed Up (H): Increases playback speed by 0.1x (e.g., from 1.5x to 1.6x).
  • Slow Down (J): Decreases playback speed by 0.1x for difficult concepts.
  • Reset Speed (L): Instantly resets your lecture to standard 1.0x normal speed.
  • Turbo Boost (Hold Spacebar): Speed up temporarily while holding the spacebar.

Pro Tip: Muscle Memory Speed Surfing

Keep your index and middle fingers hovering near H and J. When the teacher is making small talk, tap H twice. When theory starts, tap J to drop back.
