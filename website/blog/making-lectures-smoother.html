Making lectures smoother: speed fixes, focus lock, and battery saver

When you study for hours every day, small glitches get in your way. Over the past updates, we resolved stuttering sliders, fixed focus persistence, and made the player significantly lighter on laptop battery and memory.

Key Improvements

  • Smoother Speed Slider: Wider 200px bar, no mouse drag stutter, and direct typing for any exact speed.
  • Focus Lock Fixed: Focus mode now remembers your settings across episode transitions and player refreshes.
  • Battery Saver & Low RAM Mode: Reduced CPU polling so old dual-core laptops and budget devices run cool.

Direct Typing for Playback Speeds

You can now click directly on the speed number display and type an exact decimal like "1.85" without fiddling with the slider handle.

Persistent Focus State

Previously, moving to the next lecture in a batch would sometimes reset the chat sidebar. The extension now reliably maintains your focus preferences throughout your study session.
