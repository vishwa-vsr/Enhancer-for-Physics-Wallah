How to Lock Constant 720p HD Quality on Physics Wallah

Constant 720p HD locks your lecture video resolution to clear high definition right from the first second. It prevents the default video player from dropping to blurry 360p or 480p whenever brief network hiccups occur.

Key Benefits

  • Always Sharp Blackboard Notes: Never struggle to read tiny mathematical subscripts or organic chemistry bonds again.
  • Zero Blurry Buffer Drops: Overrides adaptive bitrate switches that downgrade your stream when you need clarity most.
  • Saved Across All Batches: Remembers your HD quality preference for every video so you never have to re-select it.

Why Students Need Constant HD

The standard PW video player uses adaptive bitrate streaming, which frequently starts videos at 360p and takes minutes to upgrade.

  • Unreadable Notes: Low resolutions turn handwritten board formulas and diagrams into a pixelated smear.
  • Wasted Study Time: Pausing a video and waiting for the settings gear icon to reload 720p breaks your learning momentum.
  • Consistent Visual Comfort: Sudden resolution jumps strain your eyes during 6-hour marathon study blocks.

How to Lock 720p in Enhancer

Open any lecture on pw.live, click the extension icon, and scroll to Video Quality:

  1. Constant Video Quality: Flip the toggle switch to ON. This prevents quality downgrades during small buffering events.
  2. Default Video Quality: Ensure the dropdown is set to 720p (High).
  3. Hide Quality Widget: Optionally hide the on-screen quality pill if you prefer a completely clean video frame.

Pro Tip: Fast Forward Without Quality Drops

When skipping ahead 30 seconds, default players often degrade video quality to save bandwidth. Constant HD keeps the blackboard sharp immediately after skipping.
