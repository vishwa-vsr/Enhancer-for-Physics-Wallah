Real Finish Clock: Know Exactly When Your Lecture Ends

The Real Finish Clock calculates the exact wall-clock completion time of your lecture based on your active playback speed and silence skipping, so you always know when your study session will finish.

Key Benefits

  • Accurate Wall-Clock Time: Shows real completion times like "Ends at 7:15 PM" directly on your video progress bar.
  • Dynamic Speed Math: Recalculates instantly whenever you change playback speed or enable silence skipping.
  • Better Schedule Planning: Plan meals, study breaks, and mock tests without doing mental speed arithmetic.

Why Students Need the Finish Clock

Standard video players show remaining minutes at 1.0x speed. When studying at 1.5x with pauses skipped, that remaining time is inaccurate.

  • No More Mental Arithmetic: Dividing 47 minutes by 1.35x while trying to understand organic chemistry is exhausting and imprecise.
  • Prevent Study Overrun: Never be late for coaching tests, dinner, or your next scheduled chapter.
  • Motivation to Finish: Seeing a concrete finish time (e.g. "Ends at 6:30 PM") provides a clear target to stay seated.

How to Enable and Format the Clock

Configure your clock format under the Focus tab inside the extension popup:

  • Show Finish Time: Turn on the switch to display the completion clock on your video controls bar.
  • Finish Time Format: Choose between "Minimal (Time Only)", "Clock with Label", or "Full (Clock + Remaining)".

Pro Tip: Combines with Silence Skipping

When Skip Silence is active, the Finish Clock automatically factors in dead air savings to give you an ultra-accurate completion prediction.
